Sam Sand Dunes

Golden dunes of the Thar Desert

LocationJaisalmer & Bikaner
BuiltNatural desert dunes
UNESCONo
AllowHalf a day (sunset and evening)

Overview

The Sam Sand Dunes lie in the Thar Desert about 40 km west of Jaisalmer. Rippled golden dunes roll to the horizon, and visitors come to ride camels, take jeep safaris and watch the sunset.

Many people stay overnight in desert camps, with folk music and dance after dark and a night sky free of city lights.

What to look for

  • A camel ride across the dunes
  • Sunset light on the ripples
  • Folk music and dance at camp in the evening
  • Stars in the desert sky

Visiting tips

  • Go for sunset and stay for the evening; the desert cools quickly after dark, so bring a layer.
  • Choose camps through your guide, as quality varies a lot.
  • October to February is the most comfortable season.
