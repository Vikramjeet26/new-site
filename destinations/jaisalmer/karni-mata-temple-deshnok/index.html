Karni Mata Temple, Deshnok

The famous temple of the sacred rats

LocationJaisalmer & Bikaner
BuiltMarble facade early 20th century
UNESCONo
AllowAbout 1 hour

Overview

The Karni Mata Temple at Deshnok, about 30 km from Bikaner, is dedicated to Karni Mata, a revered mystic regarded as an incarnation of the goddess Durga. It is known for the thousands of rats, called kabbas, that live in the temple and are considered sacred.

The marble facade with its silver doors dates from the early twentieth century under Maharaja Ganga Singh. Seeing a white rat is considered especially lucky.

What to look for

  • The rats running freely through the courtyard
  • The marble facade and silver doors
  • The rare white rats
  • The offerings of sweets and milk

Visiting tips

  • Remove your shoes: you will be barefoot (or in socks, if permitted) among the rats, so this is not for everyone.
  • Take care not to step on a rat; it is considered very inauspicious.
  • Dress modestly; photography rules vary, so ask.
