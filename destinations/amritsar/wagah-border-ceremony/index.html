Wagah Border Ceremony

The daily flag-lowering ceremony at the India–Pakistan border

LocationAmritsar
BuiltDaily ceremony
UNESCONo
Allow2–3 hours including travel and waiting
OpenHeld daily, about one to two hours before sunset; the start time changes with the season
Best lightLate afternoon

Overview

The Attari–Wagah Beating Retreat is a daily flag-lowering ceremony performed by India's Border Security Force and Pakistan's Rangers at the border crossing about 30 km from Amritsar. Soldiers in ceremonial uniform march, stamp and salute while crowds cheer on both sides.

The ceremony was suspended briefly in May 2025 and resumed in a scaled-down form, without the usual handshake or opening of the gates. Formats and timings can change, and the border authorities revise the start time with the season.

What to look for

  • The synchronised parade and the lowering of the two flags
  • The cheering crowds and patriotic songs
  • The border gate itself

Visiting tips

  • Arrive early, around two hours before the start on weekends and holidays, to get a seat.
  • Security is strict: carry only a small bag and a photo ID.
  • Check the current timings and format before you go, as they change with the seasons.
