Fatehpur Sikri (Buland Darwaza)

Akbar's abandoned red sandstone capital

LocationAgra & Fatehpur Sikri
BuiltAbout 1571–1585
UNESCOYes (1986)
Allow2–3 hours
OpenUsually open daily (check locally)
Best lightMorning

Overview

Fatehpur Sikri was the capital of the Mughal emperor Akbar from about 1571 until it was abandoned in the 1580s, partly because of water shortages. Its palaces, courts and the Jama Masjid are built of red sandstone.

The Buland Darwaza, the monumental gateway to the mosque, is about 54 metres high and among the tallest gateways in the world.

What to look for

  • Buland Darwaza and the Jama Masjid courtyard
  • The white marble tomb of Salim Chishti
  • Panch Mahal, the five-storey pavilion
  • Diwan-i-Khas with its central pillar

Visiting tips

  • Arrange your visit through your private guide rather than unofficial guides at the gate.
  • Allow two to three hours.
  • It fits well on the drive between Jaipur and Agra.
