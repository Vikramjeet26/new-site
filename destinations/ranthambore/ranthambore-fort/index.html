Ranthambore Fort

A hilltop fort inside a tiger reserve

LocationRanthambore, Bundi & Bharatpur
BuiltTraditionally dated to the 10th century
UNESCOYes (2013, Hill Forts of Rajasthan)
Allow1.5–2 hours
OpenAccess follows national park rules and timings (check locally)
Best lightMorning

Overview

Ranthambore Fort crowns a rocky hill inside Ranthambore National Park in Rajasthan. It is traditionally dated to the tenth century and was held by the Chauhan rulers until Alauddin Khalji of Delhi captured it in 1301.

Inside its walls are palaces, step-wells, ruined pavilions and the Trinetra Ganesh Temple, a pilgrimage site. The fort is part of the UNESCO-listed Hill Forts of Rajasthan, and its battlements look out over the forest.

What to look for

  • The Trinetra Ganesh Temple inside the fort
  • The massive gateways and ramparts
  • Views over the forest and lakes from the walls

Visiting tips

  • The fort lies inside the national park, so access follows park rules and timings; confirm before you go.
  • Wear sturdy shoes: the climb is steep and the stone is uneven.
  • Combine it with a tiger safari in the park.
