Taragarh Fort, Bundi

The "Star Fort" above Bundi's blue old town

LocationRanthambore, Bundi & Bharatpur
Built14th century, added to later
UNESCONo
Allow1.5–2 hours
OpenUsually open daily (check locally)
Best lightMorning

Overview

Taragarh Fort, the "Star Fort", crowns a steep hill above Bundi in Rajasthan. Its walls and bastions, dating from the fourteenth century and added to by later rulers, command views over the old town and the lakes around it.

Below it lies the Garh Palace, known for the painted murals of its Chitrashala, a highlight of the Bundi school of painting. Bundi is much quieter than Jaipur or Udaipur, which is part of its charm.

What to look for

  • The ruined bastions and a large cannon on the Bhim Burj
  • The view over Bundi's blue-washed old town
  • The painted murals of the Chitrashala in Garh Palace
  • The step-wells in the old town

Visiting tips

  • The climb is steep and the ruins are uneven; wear sturdy shoes.
  • Go in the morning, when it is cooler and the light is soft.
  • Combine it with Raniji ki Baori and the Garh Palace.
