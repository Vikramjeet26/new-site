Christ Church, Shimla

A neo-Gothic church on the Ridge

LocationHimachal Pradesh
Built1844–1857
UNESCONo
AllowAbout 30 minutes
OpenOpen to visitors outside service hours (check locally)
Best lightMorning

Overview

Christ Church stands on the Ridge, the open square at the heart of Shimla, and is often described as the second-oldest church in North India. Its foundation was laid in 1844 and it was consecrated in 1857.

The neo-Gothic church is known for its tall spire and its stained-glass windows, which are said to represent faith, hope, charity, fortitude, patience and humility. It is a landmark of the old colonial summer capital.

What to look for

  • The stained-glass windows
  • The spire and the pale yellow facade on the Ridge
  • The view of the hills from the Ridge

Visiting tips

  • Visit outside service hours, and dress and behave respectfully.
  • Combine it with a walk along the Mall Road and the Ridge.
  • Mornings are quieter than afternoons.
