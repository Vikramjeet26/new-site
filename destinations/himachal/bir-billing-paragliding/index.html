Bir Billing Paragliding

India's paragliding capital in the Himachal hills

Overview

Bir Billing, in Himachal Pradesh, is known as the paragliding capital of India. Pilots launch from Billing at about 2,400 metres and land in the meadows of Bir below, after flights over the Kangra valley that last roughly 15 to 30 minutes. It hosted a Paragliding World Cup in 2015.

Tandem flights with certified pilots allow first-timers to take part. Bir is also known for its Tibetan monasteries and its quiet cafes, and the surrounding mountains are a backdrop for walking and cycling.

What to look for

  • The launch at Billing and the flight over the valley
  • The landing meadows at Bir
  • The Tibetan monasteries of Bir
  • The Dhauladhar peaks in clear weather

Visiting tips

  • The best seasons are roughly March to June and October to November; the monsoon and winter are less suitable.
  • Flights depend on wind and weather; keep your schedule flexible.
  • This is an optional, extra-cost activity: choose a licensed operator through your guide and check safety standards.
