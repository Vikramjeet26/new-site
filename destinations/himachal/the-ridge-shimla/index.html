The Ridge and Mall Road, Shimla

The open heart of Shimla's colonial town

LocationHimachal Pradesh
BuiltColonial era
UNESCONo
Allow1–2 hours
OpenOpen at all hours
Best lightMorning or evening

Overview

The Ridge is the broad open promenade in the middle of Shimla, with views of the Himalaya on clear days. Around it stand Christ Church, the Town Hall and the Gaiety Theatre, with cafes and shops along the Mall Road.

It was the social heart of the British summer capital, and today it is where visitors and locals stroll, eat and watch the hills.

What to look for

  • Christ Church and its spire
  • The colonial buildings along the Mall Road
  • The Gaiety Theatre and the Town Hall
  • The view of the snow peaks on clear days

Visiting tips

  • Stroll in the morning or the evening; the Mall is closed to vehicles.
  • Combine it with Christ Church and the Jakhu Temple.
  • Be ready to walk, as much of the old town is on foot.
