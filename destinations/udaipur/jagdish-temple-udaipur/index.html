Jagdish Temple, Udaipur

A grand Vishnu temple beside the City Palace

LocationUdaipur & Chittorgarh
Built1651
UNESCONo
AllowAbout 30 minutes
OpenUsually open daily (check locally)
Best lightMorning

Overview

The Jagdish Temple, near the City Palace in Udaipur, was built in 1651 by Maharana Jagat Singh I. It is dedicated to Vishnu as Jagannath, the lord of the universe, and is built in the Indo-Aryan style.

A steep flight of 32 steps, flanked by stone elephants, leads to a tall carved spire. The walls are covered in sculptures of dancers, musicians and gods, and prayers and music continue through the day.

What to look for

  • The steps with carved elephants
  • The detailed sculpture on the spire and walls
  • The black stone image of Vishnu in the sanctum

Visiting tips

  • Remove your shoes and dress modestly.
  • It is a short stop, easily combined with the City Palace.
  • Go in the morning or at evening aarti for atmosphere.
