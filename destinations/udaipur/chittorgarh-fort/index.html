Chittorgarh Fort

Rajasthan's vast hilltop fortress of legend

LocationUdaipur & Chittorgarh
BuiltBuilt up over many centuries
UNESCOYes (2013, Hill Forts of Rajasthan)
Allow2–3 hours
OpenUsually open daily (check locally)
Best lightMorning

Overview

Chittorgarh is the largest of the six UNESCO-listed Hill Forts of Rajasthan, covering about 300 hectares on a long hill above the plains. It was the capital of the Mewar kingdom for centuries and is famous for its sieges.

Within its walls stand temples, palaces, water tanks and two victory towers, the Vijay Stambh and the Kirti Stambh. The fort holds an important place in Rajput memory as a symbol of courage and sacrifice.

What to look for

  • Vijay Stambh, the tower of victory, rising above the fort
  • Kirti Stambh, the Jain tower of fame
  • The palaces of Rana Kumbha and Padmini
  • Gaumukh reservoir, a water tank fed by a spring

Visiting tips

  • The fort is large and spread out: allow two to three hours and consider a vehicle inside.
  • Wear comfortable shoes and take water.
  • It is a rewarding stop between Udaipur and Bundi or Jaipur.
