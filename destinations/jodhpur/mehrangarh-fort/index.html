Mehrangarh Fort

The towering fort above the Blue City

LocationJodhpur, Ranakpur & Kumbhalgarh
BuiltFrom 1459
UNESCONo
Allow2–3 hours
OpenUsually open daily (check locally)
Best lightMorning

Overview

Mehrangarh is one of the largest forts in India, rising about 120 metres above Jodhpur. It was begun in 1459 by Rao Jodha, founder of the city, and its massive walls enclose palaces and a fine museum.

The old city below is painted in shades of blue, and from the ramparts you can see why Jodhpur is called the Blue City.

What to look for

  • The palace courtyards and carved jharokhas
  • The museum's howdahs, palanquins and miniature paintings
  • The view over the blue houses from the ramparts

Visiting tips

  • Allow two to three hours.
  • Visit in the morning for softer light over the blue houses.
  • Combine it with Jaswant Thada.
