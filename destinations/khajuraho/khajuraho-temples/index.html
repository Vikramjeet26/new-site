Khajuraho Temples

Chandela temples covered in sculpture

LocationKhajuraho, Orchha & Gwalior
BuiltAbout 950–1050 CE
UNESCOYes (1986)
Allow2–3 hours (Western Group)
OpenUsually open daily (check locally)
Best lightMorning

Overview

The temples of Khajuraho were built by the Chandela dynasty between about 950 and 1050 CE. The Kandariya Mahadeva, dedicated to Shiva, is the largest and most elaborate of the surviving temples, rising from a high platform in the Nagara style.

Their walls carry thousands of sculptures of gods, musicians, dancers, animals and everyday life. The famous erotic scenes are only a small part of a much wider artistic programme.

What to look for

  • The tiered shikhara of Kandariya Mahadeva, designed to echo a mountain range
  • Sculpted bands of gods, warriors and musicians
  • Lakshmana Temple, one of the earliest and best preserved
  • The light on the sandstone in the first hours of the morning

Visiting tips

  • The Western Group is the main site; allow two to three hours.
  • Light is best in the morning and late afternoon.
  • Check whether an evening light show is running during your visit.
  • It pairs well with Panna Tiger Reserve or Orchha.
