Jai Vilas Palace, Gwalior

The Scindia dynasty's grand European-style palace

LocationKhajuraho, Orchha & Gwalior
Built1874
UNESCONo
AllowAbout 1.5 hours
OpenMuseum open to visitors (check locally)
Best lightMorning

Overview

The Jai Vilas Palace in Gwalior was built in 1874 by Maharaja Jayajirao Scindia and designed by Sir Michael Filose in a blend of Italian, Tuscan and Corinthian styles. Part of it is still the residence of the Scindia family, and part is a museum.

The museum is famous for its huge Durbar Hall, with enormous chandeliers, and for its dining hall, where a silver toy train once carried food and drink around the table. It shows the splendour of a late nineteenth-century Indian princely court.

What to look for

  • The Durbar Hall and its chandeliers
  • The silver train in the dining hall
  • The museum's royal collections
  • The European-style facade and gardens

Visiting tips

  • Only part of the palace is open; allow about 90 minutes.
  • Dress comfortably; the museum is large and has stairs.
  • Combine it with Gwalior Fort for a full day in the city.
