Overview

Manikarnika Ghat is the main cremation ghat of Varanasi. For Hindus, to be cremated here beside the Ganges is believed to bring moksha, liberation from the cycle of rebirth, and fires burn at the ghat day and night.

It is a place of deep religious meaning, not a spectacle. Visitors usually see it from a boat on the river or from the lanes above, with a guide who explains the rituals and the surrounding ghats.

What to look for

  • The pyres and the ritual of cremation, seen from a respectful distance
  • The stacks of wood for the funeral pyres
  • The Manikarnika Kund, the sacred tank, above the ghat

Visiting tips

  • Photography is not permitted here; keep your camera away.
  • Be wary of anyone who asks for money for "wood donations"; it is a known scam.
  • Stay with your guide, dress modestly and keep a respectful silence.
