Red Fort

Shah Jahan's palace in Old Delhi

LocationDelhi
Built1638–1648
UNESCOYes (2007)
AllowAbout 2 hours
OpenClosed on Mondays
Best lightMorning

Overview

The Red Fort (Lal Qila) was the palace of Shah Jahan's new capital, Shahjahanabad, now Old Delhi. Its huge red sandstone walls enclose pavilions, gardens and audience halls from the height of Mughal power.

On Independence Day the Prime Minister raises the national flag from its ramparts and addresses the nation.

What to look for

  • Lahori Gate and the covered bazaar of Chatta Chowk
  • Diwan-i-Am and Diwan-i-Khas, the public and private audience halls
  • The Rang Mahal and the royal hammam
  • The small museums inside, which cover the 1857 uprising and the freedom struggle

Visiting tips

  • The Red Fort is closed on Mondays.
  • Combine it with Jama Masjid and a rickshaw ride through Chandni Chowk.
  • Security checks are thorough, so carry as little as possible.
  • Access is restricted around 15 August.
