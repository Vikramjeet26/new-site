Jal Mahal

The palace that seems to float in Man Sagar Lake

LocationJaipur & Abhaneri
Built18th century
UNESCONo
Allow20–30 minutes (viewing stop)
OpenViewed from the lakeside; the palace is not open to visitors
Best lightSunrise or sunset

Overview

Jal Mahal is a sandstone palace in the middle of Man Sagar Lake, north of Jaipur. Most of its lower storeys are said to be submerged when the lake is full.

Visitors admire it from the promenade on the lakeshore, since the palace itself is not open for tours.

What to look for

  • The reflection in still water at sunrise
  • The Aravalli hills behind the palace
  • Birds on the lake in winter

Visiting tips

  • Stop for photographs only; the palace cannot be entered.
  • It is best at sunrise or sunset.
  • It sits on the road to Amber Fort, so it is an easy stop.
