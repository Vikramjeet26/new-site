City Palace, Jaipur

The royal residence at the heart of the Pink City

LocationJaipur & Abhaneri
BuiltFrom about 1729
UNESCOWithin UNESCO-listed Jaipur city (2019)
AllowAbout 2 hours
OpenUsually open daily (check locally)
Best lightMorning

Overview

The City Palace was built by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II, founder of Jaipur, and part of it is still the royal family's residence. Its courtyards and halls mix Rajput, Mughal and European styles.

The museum holds royal costumes, weapons and textiles, and four gateways in the inner courtyard are decorated with themes of the seasons.

What to look for

  • The four gateways of Pritam Niwas Chowk
  • The huge silver urns in the Diwan-i-Khas
  • The textile and arms galleries

Visiting tips

  • Allow about two hours.
  • Go early and combine it with Jantar Mantar and Hawa Mahal nearby.
  • Part of the palace remains a private residence and is closed to visitors.
