Jantar Mantar, Jaipur

An 18th-century open-air observatory of giant instruments

LocationJaipur & Abhaneri
Built1728–1734
UNESCOYes (2010)
AllowAbout 1 hour
OpenUsually open daily (check locally)
Best lightMidday, when shadows are sharpest

Overview

Jantar Mantar is an astronomical observatory built by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II between 1728 and 1734. Its large stone and masonry instruments were designed to measure time, track the stars and predict eclipses.

The Samrat Yantra is among the largest sundials in the world, and the zodiac instruments each point to a different sign.

What to look for

  • Samrat Yantra, the giant sundial
  • The twelve zodiac instruments (Rashi Valaya)
  • The Jai Prakash Yantra

Visiting tips

  • Take a guide: the instruments mean little without explanation.
  • Visit around midday when the shadows are sharp.
  • Combine it with the City Palace next door.
