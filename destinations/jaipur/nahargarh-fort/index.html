Nahargarh Fort

The ridge fort with Jaipur's best sunset view

LocationJaipur & Abhaneri
Built1734 (Sawai Jai Singh II)
UNESCONo
Allow1–1.5 hours
OpenUsually open daily (check locally)
Best lightSunset

Overview

Nahargarh Fort sits on the edge of the Aravalli hills above Jaipur and was begun in 1734 by Sawai Jai Singh II as part of the city's defences. It looks over the Pink City, with the Jal Mahal visible in the distance.

Inside, the Madhavendra Bhawan holds a set of matching suites built for the royal queens, with painted corridors. Many visitors come at sunset for the view as the city lights come on.

What to look for

  • The view over Jaipur from the ramparts
  • The painted suites of Madhavendra Bhawan
  • The crenellated walls along the ridge

Visiting tips

  • Go for sunset, but allow time for the road down after dark.
  • Allow an hour or more.
  • Take water and a layer: the ridge can be windy.
