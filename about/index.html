Meet Vikramjeet Singh

Government-approved private guide · 20+ years in Delhi · English and Russian

India Private Guide is a private heritage travel consultancy specialising in expertly guided cultural journeys across India.

It is led by Vikramjeet Singh, a Government of India approved tour guide with over 20 years of experience in Delhi. He holds degrees in Law and Russian Literature, a rare combination that shapes how he tells the story of India's legal history, colonial architecture and living culture.

No group tours. No fixed schedules. Every itinerary is drafted by hand, refined with you and delivered by Vikramjeet personally, in English or Russian.
