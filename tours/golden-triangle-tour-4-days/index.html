The journey

The complete Golden Triangle in four days, from Old Delhi's lanes to Jaipur's forts, Fatehpur Sikri and a sunrise at the Taj.

Covers key UNESCO World Heritage locations in North India while highlighting imperial dynastic architecture, defensive fortresses, and royal residences. Full days with several long drives. Best for energetic travellers.

Highlights

  • Old Delhi rickshaw ride & city monuments.
  • Amber Fort, City Palace, and Hawa Mahal in Jaipur.
  • Fatehpur Sikri en route to Agra.
  • Taj Mahal and Agra Fort in Agra.

Route and day by day

Schematic route, not to scale.

Day 1Delhi Heritage Tour & Drive to Jaipur

Old Delhi rickshaw ride, visit Jama Masjid and Qutub Minar. Drive to Jaipur (approx. 5–6 hours). Overnight in Jaipur.

Day 2Full-Day Jaipur Sightseeing

Tour Amber Fort, Jal Mahal, City Palace, Jantar Mantar, and Hawa Mahal. Overnight in Jaipur.

Day 3Jaipur to Agra via Fatehpur Sikri

Drive to Agra (approx. 5–6 hours including stops) with en-route visit to Fatehpur Sikri (Buland Darwaza, Salim Chishti Tomb). Overnight in Agra.

Day 4Taj Mahal, Agra Fort & Return to Delhi

Sunrise tour of Taj Mahal and excursion through Agra Fort. Return drive to Delhi (approx. 3.5–4 hours).

Best time to visit

JFMAMJJASOND

Best Fair Avoid

Best time: Oct–Mar. Also good: Apr, Sep.

October to March is the comfortable season, with warm days and cool mornings. December and January can bring fog and cold at sunrise. April to June is very hot, so we start early and rest at midday. July to September is humid monsoon with fewer visitors.
