4-Day Express Golden Triangle: Delhi, Jaipur & Agra

Delhi · Jaipur · Fatehpur Sikri · Agra

Duration4 Days, 3 Nights
PaceFast pace
Journey typeExpress Escapes
PricePrivate quotation in 24 h

The journey

Covers key UNESCO World Heritage locations in North India while highlighting imperial dynastic architecture, defensive fortresses, and royal residences.

Full days with several long drives. Best for energetic travellers.

Highlights

  • Old Delhi rickshaw ride & city monuments.
  • Amber Fort, City Palace, and Hawa Mahal in Jaipur.
  • Fatehpur Sikri en route to Agra.
  • Taj Mahal and Agra Fort in Agra.

Day by day

Day 1

Delhi Heritage Tour & Drive to Jaipur

Old Delhi rickshaw ride, visit Jama Masjid and Qutub Minar. Drive to Jaipur (approx. 5–6 hours). Overnight in Jaipur.

Day 2

Full-Day Jaipur Sightseeing

Tour Amber Fort, Jal Mahal, City Palace, Jantar Mantar, and Hawa Mahal. Overnight in Jaipur.

Day 3

Jaipur to Agra via Fatehpur Sikri

Drive to Agra (approx. 5–6 hours including stops) with en-route visit to Fatehpur Sikri (Buland Darwaza, Salim Chishti Tomb). Overnight in Agra.

Day 4

Taj Mahal, Agra Fort & Return to Delhi

Sunrise tour of Taj Mahal and excursion through Agra Fort. Return drive to Delhi (approx. 3.5–4 hours).

What is included

Included

  • Private air-conditioned vehicle with driver
  • Licensed guide (English or Russian)
  • Monument entry fees
  • Hotels (3 nights)

Not included

  • Meals unless stated
  • Tips and personal expenses
  • Travel insurance

Indicative; your personal quotation confirms exactly what is included.

Good to know

  • Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays; avoid scheduling the Taj visit on a Friday.
