2-Day Khajuraho & Panna: UNESCO Temples & Tigers Express

Delhi · Khajuraho · Panna

Duration2 Days, 1 Night
PaceFast pace
Journey typeExpress Escapes
PricePrivate quotation in 24 h

The journey

Centers on the UNESCO World Heritage Khajuraho temple complex combined with an immersive tiger and wildlife safari in Panna Tiger Reserve.

Full days with several long drives. Best for energetic travellers.

Highlights

  • UNESCO Khajuraho Western and Eastern temple groups.
  • Morning jeep safari in Panna Tiger Reserve.
  • Optional Ayurvedic Wellness & Spa treatment.

Day by day

Day 1

Transit to Khajuraho & Temples Tour

Morning departure from Delhi to Khajuraho by domestic flight (flight not included; direct services are limited, so confirm the schedule before quoting). Rail is not practical for this format: the journey takes most of a day or overnight.

Guided tour of the UNESCO World Heritage Western and Eastern Groups of Temples (Kandariya Mahadeva, Lakshmana).

Optional evening Ayurvedic spa session. Overnight stay in Khajuraho.

Day 2

Panna Tiger Safari & Return to Delhi

Early morning jeep safari in Panna Tiger Reserve (approx. 1 hour from Khajuraho) along the Ken River gorges.

Return to Khajuraho for check-out and transfer to the airport for the flight back to Delhi.

What is included

Included

  • Private air-conditioned vehicle with driver
  • Licensed guide (English or Russian)
  • Monument entry fees
  • Jeep safari

Not included

  • Flights
  • Hotels (quoted to your preferred category)
  • Meals unless stated
  • Tips and personal expenses
  • Travel insurance

Indicative; your personal quotation confirms exactly what is included.

Good to know

  • Panna Tiger Reserve safari zones typically close during the monsoon (about July–September); confirm dates.
