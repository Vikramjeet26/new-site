The journey

Sunrise-fresh light on the Taj Mahal and the red sandstone courts of Agra Fort, in a single private day from Delhi, with your own guide and car.

Features two premier UNESCO World Heritage monuments (Taj Mahal and Agra Fort) and showcases Mughal royal fortress architecture and citadel design. Full days with several long drives. Best for energetic travellers.

Highlights

  • Taj Mahal & Agra Fort UNESCO sites.
  • Private day trip from Delhi in an air-conditioned vehicle.

Route and day by day

Schematic route, not to scale.

Day 1Delhi to Agra Day Journey

Early morning private pickup from Delhi and chauffeured drive to Agra via the Yamuna Expressway (approx. 3.5–4 hours each way).

Visit the Taj Mahal, experiencing the symmetry, white marble inlay work, and Mughal garden-tomb layout.

Explore the red sandstone courtyards, palaces, and halls of public/private audience at Agra Fort.

Evening return drive back to Delhi for hotel or airport drop-off.

Best time to visit

JFMAMJJASOND

Best Fair Avoid

Best time: Oct–Mar. Also good: Apr, Sep.

October to March is the comfortable season, with warm days and cool mornings. December and January can bring fog and cold at sunrise. April to June is very hot, so we start early and rest at midday. July to September is humid monsoon with fewer visitors.
