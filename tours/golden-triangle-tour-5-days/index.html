5-Day Classic Golden Triangle: Royal Rajput & Mughal Journey

Delhi · Jaipur · Abhaneri · Agra

Duration5 Days, 4 Nights
PaceModerate pace
Journey typeRoyal Fortresses & Citadels
PricePrivate quotation in 24 h

The journey

Combines UNESCO World Heritage sites in Delhi, Jaipur and Agra (Humayun's Tomb, Qutub Minar, Amber Fort, Jantar Mantar, Taj Mahal, Agra Fort) with the Chand Baori stepwell and the Nahargarh and Jaigarh hill forts on a classic five-day Golden Triangle route.

Comfortable rhythm with time at each site.

Highlights

  • Humayun's Tomb in Delhi.
  • Amber, Nahargarh, and Jaigarh Forts in Jaipur.
  • Chand Baori stepwell in Abhaneri.
  • Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, and Itimad-ud-Daulah (Baby Taj) in Agra.

Day by day

Day 1

Arrival in Delhi & City Sightseeing

Tour Humayun's Tomb and Qutub Minar. Overnight in Delhi.

Day 2

Delhi to Jaipur & City Palace

Drive to Jaipur. Tour City Palace, Jantar Mantar, and Hawa Mahal. Overnight in Jaipur.

Day 3

Jaipur Hill Forts Exploration

Full day exploring Amber Fort, Jaigarh Fort, and sunset at Nahargarh Fort. Overnight in Jaipur.

Day 4

Jaipur to Agra via Abhaneri Stepwell

Drive to Agra (approx. 6–7 hours including stops) with a stop at Chand Baori stepwell in Abhaneri. Visit Itimad-ud-Daulah (Baby Taj) in Agra if time allows; otherwise move it to Day 5 after the Taj. Overnight in Agra.

Day 5

Sunrise Taj Mahal, Agra Fort & Return to Delhi

Sunrise at Taj Mahal, tour Agra Fort, drive back to Delhi (approx. 3.5–4 hours).
