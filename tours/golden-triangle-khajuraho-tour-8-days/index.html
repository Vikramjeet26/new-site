8-Day Golden Triangle & Khajuraho: Mughal, Rajput & Chandela Heritage Trail

Delhi · Jaipur · Abhaneri · Bharatpur · Fatehpur Sikri · Agra · Morena · Gwalior · Orchha · Khajuraho

Duration8 Days, 7 Nights
PaceFast pace
Journey typeRoyal Fortresses & Citadels
PricePrivate quotation in 24 h

The journey

Links UNESCO World Heritage sites in Delhi, Agra, Jaipur and Khajuraho into a multi-state corridor across Mughal, Rajput and Chandela heritage, with Gwalior and Orchha bridging Agra and Khajuraho.

Full days with several long drives. Best for energetic travellers.

Highlights

  • Mughal monuments of Delhi and Agra (Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, Fatehpur Sikri).
  • Rajasthani forts and palaces of Jaipur, plus the Chand Baori stepwell.
  • Gwalior Fort and Orchha's Betwa riverside palaces.
  • Khajuraho UNESCO temples.

Day by day

Day 1

Delhi Arrival & Sightseeing

Tour Qutub Minar and Humayun's Tomb. Overnight in Delhi.

Day 2

Delhi to Jaipur via Abhaneri

Drive to Jaipur (approx. 5–6 hours) via Chand Baori stepwell in Abhaneri. Overnight in Jaipur.

Day 3

Jaipur Forts & City Palace

Tour Amber Fort, City Palace, Jantar Mantar, and Hawa Mahal. Overnight in Jaipur.

Day 4

Jaipur to Agra via Fatehpur Sikri

Drive to Agra via Bharatpur and Fatehpur Sikri (approx. 5–6 hours including visits). Overnight in Agra.

Day 5

Agra Monuments & Drive to Gwalior

Sunrise at Taj Mahal, tour Agra Fort. Drive to Gwalior (approx. 3–3.5 hours). Optional detour to the Chambal-region temples of Bateshwar/Mitawali near Morena adds 1.5–2 hours; recommended only on a 9-day version. Overnight in Gwalior.

Day 6

Gwalior Fort & Drive to Orchha

Tour Gwalior Fort (Man Mandir, Sas-Bahu) and Jai Vilas Palace. Drive to Orchha. Overnight in Orchha.

Day 7

Orchha Bundela Heritage & Drive to Khajuraho

Tour Jahangir Mahal, Raja Mahal, and Ram Raja Temple. Drive to Khajuraho. Overnight in Khajuraho.

Day 8

Khajuraho Temples & Departure

Guided tour of Khajuraho Western Group of Temples. Transfer to Khajuraho Airport for the flight to Delhi (flight not included) or onward journey.
