6-Day Golden Triangle & Wildlife Escape: Heritage to Bharatpur

Delhi · Jaipur · Abhaneri · Bharatpur · Fatehpur Sikri · Agra · Sikandra

Duration6 Days, 5 Nights
PaceModerate pace
Journey typeWildlife & Eco-Safaris
PricePrivate quotation in 24 h

The journey

Pairs the UNESCO World Heritage bird sanctuary of Keoladeo with UNESCO landmarks and formidable royal strongholds including Amber Fort and Agra Fort.

Comfortable rhythm with time at each site.

Highlights

  • Chand Baori stepwell in Abhaneri.
  • Keoladeo National Park rickshaw safari in Bharatpur.
  • Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, and Amber Fort.
  • Fatehpur Sikri and Akbar's Tomb at Sikandra.

Day by day

Day 1

Delhi to Jaipur

Drive from Delhi to Jaipur (approx. 5–6 hours); evening visit to Chokhi Dhani or local bazaars. Overnight in Jaipur.

Day 2

Jaipur Royal Heritage Tour

Full day tour: Amber Fort, Jal Mahal, City Palace, Jantar Mantar, Hawa Mahal. Overnight in Jaipur.

Day 3

Jaipur to Bharatpur via Abhaneri

Drive to Bharatpur, stopping at Chand Baori stepwell in Abhaneri. Check into lodge near Keoladeo National Park. Overnight in Bharatpur.

Day 4

Keoladeo Wetland Safari & Drive to Agra

Morning cycle-rickshaw birdwatching safari in Keoladeo National Park. Drive to Agra via Fatehpur Sikri (approx. 1.5–2 hours plus the visit). Overnight in Agra.

Day 5

Taj Mahal & Agra Fort

Sunrise visit to Taj Mahal and tour of Agra Fort. Overnight in Agra.

Day 6

Sikandra & Return to Delhi

Visit Akbar's Tomb at Sikandra en route back to Delhi.

Good to know

  • Taj Mahal is closed on Fridays; avoid scheduling the Taj visit on a Friday.
  • Keoladeo birdlife is best roughly October–March.
