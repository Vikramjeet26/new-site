Delhi

Duration1 Day (no overnight)

The journey

Encompasses UNESCO World Heritage landmarks (Humayun's Tomb, Qutub Minar, Red Fort) alongside sacred spiritual sanctuaries of Islam, Sikhism, and Hinduism.

A very full schedule with long driving. Choose it only if you accept a demanding day.

Highlights

  • Jama Masjid & Chandni Chowk rickshaw ride.
  • Red Fort (view), Humayun's Tomb, and Qutub Minar.
  • Gurudwara Bangla Sahib; Swaminarayan Akshardham Temple as an optional add-on.

Day by day

Day 1

Full-Day Guided Exploration of Imperial & Spiritual Delhi

Morning exploration of Old Delhi: Jama Masjid, cycle-rickshaw ride through Chandni Chowk, and view of the Red Fort.

Midday tour of South Delhi heritage sites: Humayun's Tomb and the Qutub Minar complex.

Late-afternoon spiritual visit: Gurudwara Bangla Sahib (including the community kitchen/langar).

Optional if time allows: Swaminarayan Akshardham Temple (allow 2–2.5 hours including security; closed Mondays), as a substitute for Qutub Minar or an evening add-on.

Drive past India Gate and Kartavya Path (formerly Rajpath) before hotel drop-off.

  • Monument entry fees

Not included

  • Meals unless stated

Good to know

  • Red Fort (Delhi) interior is closed on Mondays.
  • Swaminarayan Akshardham is closed on Mondays; no phones, cameras or bags are allowed inside.
  • Jama Masjid: modest dress required; visits may be restricted during prayer times.
