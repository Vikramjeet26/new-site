7-Day Temples, Ganges & Buddha Trail: Delhi to Patna

Delhi · Khajuraho · Panna · Varanasi · Sarnath · Bodh Gaya · Rajgir · Nalanda · Patna

Duration7 Days, 6 Nights
PaceModerate pace
Journey typeSpiritual & Multi-Faith
PricePrivate quotation in 24 h

The journey

Connects UNESCO World Heritage treasures of Khajuraho, Mahabodhi Temple Complex, and the excavated ruins of Nalanda University across North India.

Comfortable rhythm with time at each site.

Highlights

  • Khajuraho temples & Panna/Raneh Falls.
  • Varanasi Ganga Aarti & sunrise boat ride.
  • Mahabodhi Temple in Bodh Gaya, Nalanda University ruins, and Takht Sri Patna Sahib.

Day by day

Day 1

Delhi to Khajuraho

Flight to Khajuraho; guided tour of Western Group of Temples. Overnight in Khajuraho.

Day 2

Panna National Park & Raneh Falls

Morning tiger safari in Panna Reserve and visit Raneh Falls canyon. Overnight in Khajuraho.

Day 3

Khajuraho to Varanasi

Transit to Varanasi by flight (flights not included; see the routing note on IND-08); check in and attend evening Ganga Aarti. Overnight in Varanasi.

Day 4

Varanasi Rituals & Sarnath

Dawn boat ride on the Ganges; visit Sarnath archaeological site and museum. Overnight in Varanasi.

Day 5

Varanasi to Bodh Gaya

Drive to Bodh Gaya (approx. 5.5–6 hours); tour UNESCO Mahabodhi Temple complex. Overnight in Bodh Gaya.

Day 6

Rajgir & Nalanda University Ruins

Drive to Rajgir (Griddhakuta / Vulture Peak) and tour UNESCO Nalanda ruins. Drive to Patna (approx. 2.5 hours). Overnight in Patna.

Day 7

Patna Sahib & Flight to Delhi

Visit Takht Sri Patna Sahib; return flight to Delhi (flight not included).
